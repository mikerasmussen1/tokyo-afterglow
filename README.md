# Tokyo Afterglow

Two illustrated hidden-object adventures: a Tokyo street at dusk and an autumn journey through Kyoto. Find 18 objects in each map.

## Play

[Play Tokyo Afterglow](https://mikerasmussen1.github.io/tokyo-afterglow/)

- Drag the scene to explore. Pinch or double-tap to zoom.
- All 18 shapes are shown in the key. Tap a shape to see its name.
- Hint moves closer and gently circles a target. Hints recharge in six seconds.
- There are no wrong-tap penalties. Best times are saved only in your browser.
- Choose either level from the menu, or continue to Kyoto after finishing Tokyo.

You can also download and open `index.html` offline. The game is self-contained and needs no account or external libraries.

## Files

The finished scene plates, separate object artwork and shape keys are in `assets/v7/`. Interface sources and item coordinates are included. Run `python3 build_v7.py` to rebuild `index.html` after changing these files.

Version: v7-tokyo-kyoto.
